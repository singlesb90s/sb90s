Dating apps are fine. Doing something together is better.

SB90s hosts in-person events for single people born in the 90s. Each one is organized around an activity, like climbing or yoga, so you meet people while doing something you care about instead of making small talk across a table.

Every event has a theme and a crowd that fits it. You'll show up already sharing something with the people around you, and the conversation tends to take care of itself.

Five events. Five kinds of people.

Pick the ones that sound like you. Come to all of them if you want.

Climbers

An evening at a climbing gym. Beginners are welcome, and nobody needs to be good at it.

Fitness

For people who train and want to meet someone who gets it.

Yoga & spiritual

A slower, more reflective night for people who value mindfulness and growth.

Entrepreneurs

For founders, freelancers and people building something of their own.

Ecstatic dance

Movement, music and contact improv for people who like to let loose.
